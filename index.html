Why you need us?

Over the years, AI has gone from needing endless prompting and iteration to turning ideas into working products in a matter of hours. With AI-assisted development and vibecoding, building complex applications has become easier and more accessible than ever. And now, everyone is doing it, ideas are being built, launched, and put in front of real users faster than ever before.

But there's a catch. As building has become easier, the experience has often become an afterthought. We're seeing more products that work well but feel generic, unpolished, soulless, or directionless. The technology might be impressive underneath, but users only experience what's in front of them, and that experience shapes whether they trust the product.

That's the gap I try to bridge as a designer: taking products that simply work and turning them into experiences that feel intuitive, polished, intentional, and distinct. Because building the product is easier now. Making it feel genuinely well made is where the real work begins.
